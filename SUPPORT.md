## Before you report

1. **Make a debug report.** In Leonida: *About & help* → **Report a problem…** (or `leonida report`). It saves
   `Leonida-report-<date>.zip` on your Desktop with the logs, install state and system info, with your user name
   and private paths removed, then opens the bug form with your system info filled in. Attach the zip to the issue.
2. After a game crash, make the report **before** starting the game again, so the crash files are still the newest.
3. Check [known crashes and in-game checks](docs/IN-GAME-CHECKS.md) and the open issues.

## What we can't help with

- **GTA Online.** Leonida is story mode only. Turn **Online-safe** on (the *Manage* page) before going online.
- Bugs inside a mod itself: please tell its author (the mod's card in Leonida links to the author's page).
- Paid mods' downloads: those come from the author's store or Patreon.
- Pirated copies of the game.

Leonida V is made by volunteers in their free time, so an answer can take a few days. Thanks for your patience.
