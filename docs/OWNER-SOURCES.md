# The owner's sources

Every mod in the sources the owner gave, what Leonida does with it, and the author's own page. The owner's rule
(2026-11-25): one modpack, **Leonida (owner's pick)**, built only from these sources:
- no alternatives or replacements of our own;
- no duplicates that break each other;
- paid mods offered as "buy to enable";
- never a re-upload or leak.

Sources checked on 2026-11-25. The mod lists come from the videos' own descriptions. Re-checked on 2026-11-26:
- **The video descriptions:** all five read in full, plus the pinned comments. No mod is missing; fbicat's HUD is in
  PV1 too.
- **The "out" mods:** looked up again on the authors' own pages only. None has an Enhanced version yet; three are
  marked "watch".

**Strictly required tools,** each needed by mods above:
- **Script Hook V:** every script mod.
- **Script Hook V .NET Enhanced:** the HUD and every .NET script. It's also in PV1/PV2's tools.
- **LemonUI:** the HUD, Benny's, Street Food and Enable All Interiors. It's also in PV2's tools.
- **OpenRPF:** every mod that goes into the `mods` folder. It's also in PV2's tools.
- **DirectStorageFix:** recommended for Enhanced on SHVDNE's page.
- **Resource Extensions:** listed as a requirement by Map Updates' readme. It's also in IN2.

Script Hook V's `dinput8.dll` / `xinput1_4.dll` is the .asi loader.

**Straight To Story Mode** is also in the base as a **tool Leonida needs, not a replacement for anything**. The play
test, `leonida playtest --repeat` and the launcher's Play button need the game to go straight into story mode.

The pack comes in two variants, **16 GB of RAM** (the default) and **32 GB of RAM**. They set only Resource
Extensions' memory budgets and crowd density; docs/SETTINGS.md has the values.

- **NVE** replaces Drive V with *Drive V for NVE*, skips OpenRPF (NVE uses its onigiri folder), and moves the other

Traffic V (docs/CONFLICTS.md). `leonida paid list` still shows them.
